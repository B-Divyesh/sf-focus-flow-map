Read how your focus data stays local.

Recording a focus route does not send the page, its content, or your interaction history to us.

What the extension stores

Recording begins only after you choose Start recording.

The extension stores redacted element descriptions, page position, direction, timestamps, and review notes.

It never reads or stores input values.

URLs exclude queries and hashes. Sensitive path values, email addresses, web addresses, and token-like text are redacted.

What leaves your device

No focus route is sent to us.

If you verify Pro, the license token alone goes to Sociobot’s billing API over HTTPS.

This site uses no analytics, advertising pixels, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts.

How the demo stays separate

The sample demo uses the demo:focus-flow-map: browser-storage namespace.

It does not read or change your real license data. Resetting or leaving the demo removes its state.

Your controls

You can delete any session from the route report.

Uninstall the extension to remove its saved data. Clear this site’s saved license token to remove it.

You control exported files after download.

Permissions

activeTab and site access let the recorder observe focus only where you start it.

storage keeps route reports locally. tabs identifies the active page and opens the route report.

Contact and changes
